About this page

Every entry is taken from Tables I–X of: Poli MC, Aksentijevich I, Bousfiha A, et al. Human inborn errors of immunity: 2024 update on the classification from the International Union of Immunological Societies Expert Committee (final update of the 2024 report, January 2025).

Entries marked “New 2024” are the ones printed in bold in the report as additions since the 2022 update. Where the report merges table cells across several genes, the shared text is repeated on each gene's entry. The page number shown on each entry points back to the report so you can check the original wording.

The whole tool is one file and works offline. Links to OMIM, NCBI and ClinVar open those external sites.

This is a look-up aid. It does not give medical advice.
